Privacy Policy

Hidden Everyday Facts Uploader is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What the tool accesses

The tool uses the YouTube Data API with the scope youtube.upload, only to upload videos to the owner's channel.

What we collect and store

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. The only credential stored is the owner's own OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub account.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
